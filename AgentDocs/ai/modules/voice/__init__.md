This component serves as the package initialization point for all voice-related functionalities within the AI system. It defines the namespace for voice processing, speech bridging, and auditory interaction modules.

As a sub-package of the broader AI modules, it provides the structural entry point for integrating audio capabilities into the system architecture. It is designed to house specialized modules such as [speech_bridge.py](/AgentDocs/ai/modules/voice/speech_bridge.md) and [vibe_module.py](/AgentDocs/ai/modules/voice/vibe_module.md), which handle the translation between auditory input/output and the core AI logic.
